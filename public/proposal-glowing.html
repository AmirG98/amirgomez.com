02

What We Propose

Three tracks. Take one, take two, take all three. They work together but none of them depends on the others.

Track A

Paid: Meta + Google together

These two do different jobs and that is the whole point of running them at the same time.

  • Google catches the people already looking. Somebody typing "house cleaning near me" has their wallet out. That is the cheapest booking you will ever get.
  • Meta creates the ones who were not looking yet. Before and after photos, a real person talking to camera, the neighborhood you serve. That is what makes somebody who had not thought about it book a clean.
  • Structured creative testing. Several angles running at once, judged after three or four days, cut what loses and scale what wins. Not one ad hoping.
  • Local targeting by zip code, so you are not paying to reach people outside your service area.

Running one without the other is the most common mistake in this category. Google alone caps out at existing demand. Meta alone pays to teach people something Google would have told them for less.

Track B

Organic: the part that keeps paying after you stop

Paid stops the day you stop paying. This does not.

  • Google Business Profile done properly. For a local service this is the highest-leverage asset you own, and it is usually the most neglected. Reviews, photos, service areas, posts, questions answered.
  • Local SEO so you show up in the map pack for your neighborhoods, not just your city.
  • Content production for Instagram, built from the work you are already doing. Before and afters are the single best performing content in this business and you are already creating them every day.
  • A cadence you do not have to think about. We produce, you approve, it goes out.
Track C

Website: stop losing the traffic you already paid for

Every click that lands on a page that does not convert is money you already spent and threw away.

  • A site built for one action: book a clean or request a quote. Nothing else competing for attention.
  • Fast on a phone, because that is where the booking happens.
  • Proof above the fold: reviews, before and afters, service area, pricing clarity.
  • Tracking installed properly, so from day one we know which ad produced which booking. Right now you cannot know that.

If the budget only stretches to two tracks, this one plus paid beats paid alone every time. There is no point driving traffic to a page that leaks.

How It Would Run

Assuming you start with paid plus the site, which is what I would do in your position.

Weeks 1–2
Setup and tracking. Google Business Profile cleaned up, conversion tracking installed, Google Ads account built with real keyword architecture, first round of Meta creative produced. By the end of week two you can see which click turned into a call.
Weeks 3–6
Learning. Several creative angles running at once, cut and scaled every three or four days. The goal here is not profit, it is finding out what works. Expect the cost per booking to move a lot.
Weeks 7–12
Scaling what won. Budget concentrates behind the angles that produced bookings. This is where the cost per booking settles and we can tell you what each new customer actually costs.
Month 4 onward
Organic starts carrying weight. If Track B is running, this is roughly when the map pack and the content begin producing bookings you did not pay for.
