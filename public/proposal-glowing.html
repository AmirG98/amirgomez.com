What We Propose

Quoted separately so you can start with one and add the rest when it earns it. Different processes, different people, different timelines.

Quote 1

Meta + Google Ads together

This can run on its own, without the website refresh and without SEO, and still produce. The two platforms feed each other: Meta creates demand, Google captures it.

  • Meta, built properly. Not one promoted post. Several creative angles running at once, judged every three or four days, cut what loses and scale what wins. Target: bring your $75 cost per lead down.
  • Google search campaigns, which you are not running today. Keyword research first, then campaigns that capture people actively searching for cleaning in your neighborhoods.
  • Remarketing. Ads that follow people who hit your site, or engaged with the Meta ad, or inquired once and never converted. Several data sources feeding the display network. This is pure upside because you have none of it today.
  • LSA review and optimization. You are spending $4,000 a month there. We look at what it is doing and where it leaks.
  • Creative production from what your team already shoots. Your cleaners take before and after photos in the home. We edit, refine and turn those into content. If we need video, we give you and your assistants specific instructions on what to capture, then we handle the editing.

On UGC: the goal is content that does not look like an ad. For another client we deliberately make them a little rough so they read as organic and do not get scrolled past. Same approach here, built from real cleans in real homes.

Quote 2

SEO, quoted separately

Almost a different discipline with a different expert executing it. This is the part that actually brings your cost per lead down, because it does not stop when you stop paying.

  • Monthly content and blogs, built from keyword research, targeting what your buyers actually type.
  • Longtail strategy. Going after very specific phrases with very specific content, so leads start trickling in without you paying for each one.
  • Backlinks and technical structure, the off-page half of ranking.
  • Google Business Profile posting and upkeep, which you are already doing with before and after photos. We make it systematic.
  • Built for AI answers too, not just blue links. Where people ask an engine who to hire, you want to be the answer.
Quote 3

Website refresh, quoted separately

You built the current site with your assistant. If we are going to send paid traffic to it, it needs refining first.

  • Built for one action: book a clean or request a quote. Nothing competing for attention.
  • SEO best practices baked in, because SEO lives inside the website. The on-page half of ranking gets done here.
  • Fast on a phone, which is where the booking happens.
  • Proof above the fold: reviews, before and afters, service area.
  • Tracking installed properly, so we know which ad produced which booking. You cannot know that today.

If the budget only stretches to two, this one plus Quote 1 beats Quote 1 alone. There is no point paying for clicks that land on a page that leaks.
