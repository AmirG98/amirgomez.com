High-ticket lending deals from a webinar funnel

$65M in pipeline generated in 6 months, at $80 per qualified appointment.

ChallengeReach brokers and borrowers considering large lending decisions, where nobody buys from a click and every deal closes through a conversation.

What was doneA campaign Amir personally led for this ground-up construction funding company: complete funnel built around on-demand webinars, qualifying each lead before any human time is spent on it. Giant Partners B2B Google + Microsoft Ads A+ Growth client work

Channel revenue 3.7x from 2022 to 2024. Return on ad spend doubled, 1.74 to 3.76.

ChallengeWe inherited Giant Partners' Google Ads and Microsoft Ads accounts with a +15% year-over-year revenue target and no clean structure to build on.

What was doneEighteen months of systematic funnel work inside the PPC platforms, separating volume strategies at the top of the funnel from conversion strategies at the bottom. No dramatic swings, controlled iteration.

Why it matters to youThis is what method looks like over time: compounding improvement you can audit quarter by quarter, not spikes you cannot explain.

The model we would run for you

Ad · VSL · Conversion

For a considered, trust-based purchase like merchant services, the structure that consistently performs is simple: the ad opens the door, a VSL does the explaining and the qualifying, and the conversion step arrives pre-sold. The VSL can also be an on-demand webinar, same mechanism with longer form. The lending case above runs exactly this model.

Step 1Meta ad

Reaches the right business owner with a specific promise. Its only job is the click, not the sale.

→
Step 2VSL page

A focused video page that explains, builds trust and filters. Or an on-demand webinar for longer arguments.

→
Step 3Conversion

A booked call or application from someone who already understands the offer. The closer's time is protected.
