What the spend has not told us yet. Both channels are buying attention efficiently: a 3.4% CTR is strong for LinkedIn, and $0.43 per landing page view is low for a B2B accounting audience. What is missing is the layer that connects that traffic to a lead. Without the Meta pixel and CRM integration, we can see traffic arriving but cannot attribute a single lead or dollar to it. Installing the pixel is the top priority this week, and it is what will make the next version of this table show cost per lead instead of cost per click.
