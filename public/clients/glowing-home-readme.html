Onboarding

Six things to get us running. The first three are what block us from starting the audit, so those come first.

What we need from you

1

Meta Business Manager access Blocks the audit

Go to business.facebook.com → Settings → Users → Partners → Add partner, and send us the invite with Admin access on the ad account and the page.

The ad account stays in your name, with your payment method. We come in as a partner, so the history and the data stay on your side even if we stop working together.

2

Google Ads and Local Service Ads Blocks the audit

In Google Ads: Tools → Access and security → invite us with Admin. For LSA, the access comes through the same Google account.

If LSA is on a separate login, send us that one too. We need to see the $4,000 a month working, not guess at it.

3

Google Business Profile and Analytics Blocks the audit

Manager access on the Business Profile, and viewer or admin on Google Analytics and Search Console if you have them set up.

If you do not have Analytics installed, say so and we will set it up during week one.

4

Shared Drive folder

We will send you a Google Drive folder. Drop in anything you already have: logos, brand files, photos of completed jobs, before and afters, reviews, pricing sheets, your service area list.

The before and after photos matter more than you think. They are the best performing content in this category and your cleaners are already taking them.

5

Brand questionnaire

A set of questions about your offer, your customers and how you talk about the business. It takes about 20 minutes and it saves weeks of back and forth on every ad and page we write after.

We send it once the contract is signed.

6

Jobber and your booking flow

View access to Jobber, or at minimum a walkthrough of how a lead goes from form to booked job. We need to see where leads land and how your team follows up.

This is also where we look at the connection between your forms and the CRM, which is usually where leads quietly leak.

Thumbtack and Slack. Send us read access to Thumbtack so we can see what those leads cost you, and add amir@amirgomez.com to your Slack as a guest. Slack is where the day to day happens.

WhenWhat we do

What stays the same for now

Keep your budgets exactly where they are while we audit:

  • Meta: $25 a day minimum. Below that there is not enough data to tell a good ad from a bad one.
  • Google LSA: around $4,000 a month. It is working, so we do not touch it until we understand it.
  • Thumbtack: $700 to $1,000. Same reasoning.

You can stop your own Instagram posting whenever you want to hand that over. Ad spend keeps going directly to the platforms, never through us.
