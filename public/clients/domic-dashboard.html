What we understand so far

Tell us where this is wrong

The business

High-end home automation and integrated control: lighting, climate, security, audio and automation behind a single interface. Over 300 projects across eight years in Latin America, now expanding into the United States from Miami.

Three audiences that do not buy the same way: homeowners, corporate offices and luxury multi-residential developments.

What makes Domic different

The philosophy is giving the client back control through a transparent process. People decide what their system actually needs, informed, instead of being sold products they never asked for.

Selling the way of living, not the equipment. The technical sale stays separate from the technical execution.

The problem to solve

Quoting is expensive and slow when the person on the other side is not qualified. Technical time gets spent on projects that were never viable.

The fix is filtering investment expectations at the very first interaction, before anyone books an engineer's afternoon.

How the offer is structured

The calculator lets a visitor shape their own scope and watch the cost move with their priorities. In the demo, a 10,000 sqft project in Miami Beach went from USD 846,000 to USD 344,000 by adjusting what mattered.

From there, the USD 7,000 engineering service is what gets the design work paid for before execution starts.

One thing we will not put in front of the visitor. The internal estimate works off roughly 2% of the property value, and that reasoning stays behind the scenes. Showing it anchors the visitor's perception before the conversation happens, and turns away leads that would have qualified with the right handling.

How we work together

So nothing depends on remembering to ask
Weekly
Written update. What moved, what is blocked and what is coming. Sent even on quiet weeks, because silence is the thing that makes engagements feel adrift.
Every two weeks
Working call, 30 to 45 minutes. We walk through results, decide what to change and agree on the next two weeks. Once campaigns are running, this is where budget decisions get made.
Monthly
Performance report. Spend by channel, what it produced, what we learned and what changes because of it. Written to be read in five minutes.

Anytime
Direct line. Anything urgent goes straight to us, no ticket, no queue.
What we need from you to keep this rhythm. Two things, and both are small: feedback on lead quality once calls start coming in, since we cannot see what happens on your side of the phone, and a decision on anything marked as waiting here. Everything else is on us.

How results get measured

Qualified calls, not traffic
Qualified sales callsThe number that matters
People whose investment range already matches what Domic does, booked and showing up. A thousand visitors who cannot afford the service are worth less than ten who can.
Cost per qualified callEfficiency
Total media spend divided by calls that were worth having. This is what tells us whether to scale a channel or shut it off.
Calculator completionsIntent
Visitors who went far enough to shape their own scope. A strong signal even when they do not book right away.
Engineering services soldRevenue
The USD 7,000 step. The closest thing to a true return figure, and the one that tells us the funnel is doing its job end to end.
Spend by channelTransparency
Every dollar, split by platform, with what it produced next to it. Reported whether the number is good or bad.
What we will not report as a win. Impressions, reach, followers and click-through rates on their own. They are useful for diagnosing a campaign, and we will show them when they explain something, but they are not results. If a month goes badly, the report will say so.
