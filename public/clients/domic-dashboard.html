Why setup comes before spend. Sending traffic to a funnel that cannot qualify or measure is how agency budgets disappear without a trace. Every dollar of media will land on a page that captures the visitor, a calculator that filters them, and a CRM that records what happened. Until those three are in place, spending is guessing.
