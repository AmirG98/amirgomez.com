Targets we agreed on

  • Meta cost per lead from $75 to under $50 in the first quarter. This is the number we are held to.
  • Measure down to booked jobs and profit, not just cost per lead. Chris raised this and we agreed: a cheap lead that never books is not a win.
  • Budgets stay as they are while we audit. Meta at $25/day minimum, LSA around $4,000, Thumbtack unchanged.

What Chris said that matters

On his own experience: he does not have a background in content or growth, and said so directly. He is not looking to learn it, he is looking to hand it over.

On the market: Orange County is competitive on volume but, in his words, the other cleaning businesses are not buttoned up and are not doing marketing professionally. He sees room to grow because of that gap, not despite it.

On the booking page: the Jobber booking page is where he ultimately wants traffic to land. Our position is that it needs to look and feel like the rest of the site, and that we capture name and phone before the full form, so we can follow up with people who abandon it.

On posting: his assistants currently use AI to build and post content. He can hand that over whenever he wants to free them up.
