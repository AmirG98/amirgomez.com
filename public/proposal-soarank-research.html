01

Key Findings

01

The category is well funded, and the leaders sell proof, not GEO education

Profound raised a $180M Series D at a $1.8B valuation on Sep 15, 2026, seven months after a $96M Series C, and serves 1,000+ enterprise brands. Peec AI reported $10M ARR 16 months after launch and 2,500+ customers. Their homepages show product and results; soarank.com spends most of its page explaining the problem.[1][2][6][7]

02

Winners show the product working in the first screen; SoaRank does not

Profound (AI Marketer workspace), Peec (dashboard with a real customer, Attio), Otterly (brand report with competitors), Evertune (AI Brand Score dashboard) and Rankscale (dashboard right below the hero) all show product UI up front. soarank.com shows an input field and a Maya persona card; the product only appears in a video further down.[3][9][11][21][27][29]

03

Proof is named customers with numbers, not famous brands with disclaimers

Profound lists 21 customer stories (Plaid 300% referral traffic growth, Ramp 7x AI visibility). AthenaHQ quotes named CMOs (Gruns share of voice 2.0% to 12.6% in 60 days). Goodie shows SteelSeries, Rathbones and a Unilever brand with percentages. soarank.com leads with Nike, Tesla and Netflix reports marked 'not a SoaRank customer' and first-name-only testimonials, including one from its own founder.[5][14][24][29]

04

A free one-time AI visibility score is now a commodity

HubSpot's free, no-account AI Search Grader scores a brand out of 100 using OpenAI, Perplexity and Gemini, almost exactly SoaRank's free scan. AthenaHQ offers a 10-minute free audit and a free plan, Otterly lists seven free GEO tools, Goodie offers an AI Visibility Index, site audit and llms.txt generator.[28][14][11][24]

05

SoaRank's $99 entry sits in a crowded band, but the done-for-you small-business lane is mostly unclaimed

Entry prices: Otterly $29, Peec about $80 (annual), Rankscale $99, Scrunch $250 to $300, AthenaHQ free then $295, Goodie $399, Evertune $800, Profound enterprise after a trial. Nearly all target marketing teams, agencies or enterprise; none positions as an AI CMO doing the work for founders and small businesses.[12][10][27][17][14][25][22][4]

06

Trust signals are standard among leaders and missing at SoaRank

Profound (SOC 2, SCIM, RBAC), AthenaHQ (SOC 2 Type 2, GDPR, NIST CSF, Trust Center), Scrunch (SOC 2 Type II) and Goodie (Security & Compliance page). Evertune and Rankscale publish named team pages. soarank.com has no security, data handling, about or team page.[3][14][16][24][21][27][29]

07

Leaders publish research that gets cited, which is how they show up in roundups and AI answers

Profound Index and AEO report, AthenaHQ State of AI Search 2026, Otterly GEO experiments, Goodie Research Lab and AI Visibility Index, Evertune AI Search Statistics. Of seven 2026 roundups reviewed, Peec appeared in six and Profound was ranked in four (benchmark in a fifth); SoaRank in none. Several roundups are vendor-written and rank the author first or high (Superlines, Clearscope, NoGood for Goodie).[3][14][11][24][21][30][31][32][33][34][35][37]

04

What This Means for SoaRank's Website

  1. Put Maya to work above the fold: replace the gradient card with a real scan result (engines checked, score, competitors AI recommends instead, first actions). Five of eight competitors show product UI in or right below the hero.
  2. Make the free scan worth more than HubSpot's free 0 to 100 grader: show the exact prompts where competitors win, make the report shareable, and bridge directly to what Copilot ($99) fixes this week. Design scan, result, email, plan as the main conversion path.
  3. Swap famous-brand reports for customer proof: before and after scores, timeframe, full name, company and photo or logo. Remove the founder's own testimonial; keep famous-brand reports only as a sample report section.
  4. Own the lane the leaders are not in: an AI CMO that does the work for founders and small businesses. Order the page how it works first, pricing after.
  5. Add the trust layer US buyers expect: About and team page, a data handling and security page, and a short methodology page.
  6. Make the site its own best GEO case study: a recurring research asset (e.g. AI visibility index for US small businesses), comparison pages (vs Otterly, vs HubSpot AEO Grader) and a best GEO tools for small business page, to get into roundups and AI answers.
