01

What We Saw on soarank.com

First look
It reads as a template. Orange on cream with oversized type, and a gradient card standing in for Maya. A US founder decides in a few seconds whether this is a funded product or a side project, and today the design does not help that call.
The product
Maya is never shown working. The report and the weekly re-test are the reasons to buy, and today they live in a video halfway down the page.
Order
Pricing comes before "How it works". The page asks for a plan before it explains what the plan does.
Proof
The reports lean on Nike, Tesla and Netflix, with a disclaimer that they are not customers. Testimonials carry first names only. For B2B buyers in the US, one real customer story with numbers beats six famous logos.
Trust
No team, no company page, nothing on how brand data is handled. For a product that analyzes your brand, this is the question every buyer has and nobody on the page answers.
AI search
A GEO company's own site should be the best example of GEO. Schema and answer-ready content are part of the pitch here, not a technical detail.
The product and most of the copy stay. What changes is the presentation and the order, plus proof that holds up under a skeptical read.
